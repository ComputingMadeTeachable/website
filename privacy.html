Privacy policy

Your information, kept simple.

We collect as little as we can, use it only for what you asked for, and never sell it. Here's the detail.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

Computing Made Teachable is run by Dr Neferti Luzuriaga, who is responsible (the "data controller") for the personal information described here. You can contact us at hello@computingmadeteachable.com.

We don't collect information about students, and you should never send us students' personal data.

Our emails

We use a confirmation step (double opt-in), so nobody can sign you up without your say-so. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one click removes you. Our email provider records whether emails are opened and which links are clicked, which helps us send fewer, more useful emails.

Who we share it with

We only share information with trusted services that help us run the site, and only what they need:

  • Brevo: sends our emails and stores the mailing list.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and protects it from attacks.
  • Payhip, with Stripe or PayPal: process purchases and deliver files.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts on this site, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

If you buy through a marketplace such as Tes, that marketplace's own privacy policy applies to your purchase. Some of these services may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use recognised legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We never sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Cookies

This website doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary security cookies to protect the site, and these don't need your consent.

How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe, or after two years of not opening any emails, whichever comes first.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires, usually six years.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to help you, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop using it for emails (or simply click unsubscribe)

Just email hello@computingmadeteachable.com. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be emailed to subscribers.
